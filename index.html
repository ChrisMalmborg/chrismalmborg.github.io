About

Where financial services
meets AI.

I started my career in an unexpected place, studying Music Industry at USC with coursework across both the Thornton School of Music and the Marshall School of Business. That combination of business fundamentals and creative storytelling taught me how to sell ideas and build relationships before I ever entered financial services.

Today I'm an AVP of Business Development at LPL Financial, where I help financial advisors navigate technical platform migrations. I've personally closed 40+ complex, multi-stakeholder deals representing over $1.9B in assets under management, earned two promotions in three years, and consistently rank in the top third nationally.

Along the way I developed the core belief that the wealth management industry is ripe for AI transformation. So I taught myself to build. Using Claude Code, I've shipped production applications that integrate with APIs, process data, and deliver real value.

I'm looking for the intersection — a role where deep domain expertise in financial services meets the frontier of AI.
